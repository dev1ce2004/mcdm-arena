# MCDM Arena: Privacy Notice

Version 1, 19 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data MCDM Arena handles, why, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Who is responsible

Tin Šolić, Croatia, is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: **mcdm.arena@gmail.com**.

## Without a team: nothing leaves your computer

The App stores your games, bots, results and settings only on your computer (in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MCDMArena`). The author does not receive them. The App has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

One exception: to tell you about new versions, the App asks GitHub for the latest release of the public download page. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any visit to a website; the App sends nothing else.

## With a team (optional)

If you sign in to a team, the team service processes:

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your email address and an account id | To sign you in (a code is emailed to you) and to show teammates who shared what | Performing the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Your teams and your role in each | To decide what you may see and do | Same |
| What you choose to share: bots, comparison results, Crisis Lab sets, game summaries, re-check results | To show them to your teammates | Same |
| Sign-in records (time, IP address, device information) kept by the hosting provider | Security and abuse prevention | Legitimate interest in a secure service (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

Only members of a team can see what is shared in that team. Game summaries contain game statistics, not personal data, apart from who shared them.

## Who processes it for the author

- **Supabase** (database, file storage and sign-in), hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
- **Google** (Gmail), which sends the sign-in and invitation emails.
- **GitHub**, which hosts the download page and answers the update check.

They act on the author's behalf under their own data-processing terms. Some may transfer data outside the EU under the safeguards the GDPR requires (such as standard contractual clauses).

## How long

Your account data is kept while you have an account. Shared items are kept until their uploader or the team owner deletes them, or the team is deleted. If you ask for your account to be deleted, your account and email address are removed within 30 days; items you shared may be removed with it or kept without your email, as you choose. Hosting providers keep their security logs for a limited time under their own policies.

## Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to **mcdm.arena@gmail.com**. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority; in Croatia that is the Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP, azop.hr).

## Children

The team service is not meant for children under 16.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the new version comes with the App and on the download page, with a new date.
